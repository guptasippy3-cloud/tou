# Asset notes

- The triangle character and interface are SVG/CSS/HTML assets developed for this prototype with AI assistance.
- Generated care audio is produced by `scripts/make-storybook.py` and `scripts/make-foley.py`; magic audio by `scripts/make-magic.py`; the current voiced dance approximation by `scripts/make-dance.py`.
- Generated sound effects are original approximations, not licensed film recordings.
- `public/audio/pip-voice.mp3` and `pip-voice-clean.wav` predate this portfolio preparation. Their provenance and redistribution rights must be confirmed before public upload. They are excluded from this public snapshot and its HTML/service-worker references. The local installed test build is a separate artifact.
- Fonts: DM Sans and Manrope, installed through the corresponding Fontsource packages. Preserve their included licenses when distributing.

Do not publish unclear-provenance recordings as part of a public repository or release until their rights are confirmed.
